# Your daily call list — quick start

**The whole job: open the app → press the big green button → call → tap how it went. Repeat.**

## Every day
1. Open the link and click **🎯 Work leads**.
2. Type your first name in the **Your name** box (once). It fills in your scripts.
3. You'll see **one business at a time**. Do these two steps:
   - **Step 1 · Call** — tap the green button (on a phone it dials). Ask for the person shown.
   - **Step 2 · How did it go?** — tap one button. The next business appears automatically.
4. That's it. Your progress bar shows how many you've done today.

## If you're not sure what to say
Read the quoted line under **Say this**, then pick **one** question. You don't need all three.

## The buttons
| Button | Use it when |
|---|---|
| ✅ Talked to them | You spoke to someone |
| 📞 No answer / left voicemail | Nobody picked up |
| 🔁 Call back later | They asked you to call back — put the day/time in the note box first |
| ❌ Not a fit | Wrong business, not interested, or a chain |
| ⏭ Skip for now | You'd rather do a different one first (it comes back next time) |

**Made a mistake?** Tap **↩ Undo last** (top right).
**Callbacks and no-answers** are saved under **Follow-ups** at the bottom. Tap one to reopen it.

## Good to know
- Your work **saves automatically** in this browser. Closing the tab, refreshing, or taking a break loses nothing.
- Use the **same browser on the same computer** each day — that's where your history lives.
- Names/emails come from the business's own website, so a name is sometimes missing or wrong. If you hear "that's not who you want," just tap **Not a fit** or leave a note and move on.
- **⚠️ Chain / franchise** warning = the decision is made at corporate. Skip it.
- Want a record? Tap **⬇️ Download my call log** to get a spreadsheet of everything you did.
- Need more leads? Tap **🚀 Find new businesses**, pick a state, press the blue button, and wait. New leads are added to your queue.

## Tips that help (use what works for you)
- Set the **Goal** to something small first (5). Hitting it feels good; raise it later.
- Don't read the "More about them" section unless you want to — it's optional.
- Ignore the queue count. Only ever look at the one in front of you.
